I am a Master's student in Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Hyderabad. My work bridges the gap between theoretical physics, computational simulations, and hands-on hardware engineering.

Courses & Academics

  • Mathematical Physics: Advanced study of complex variables, Green's functions, and Sturm-Liouville problems.

  • Computational Methods: Application of numerical schemes to solve partial differential equations in physics.

Projects

1. Cosmic Rays Propagation in Galaxy

Developed a comprehensive simulation of galactic cosmic ray transport. This project heavily utilized computational techniques, including Monte Carlo methods and Crank-Nicolson PDE solvers, to model advection-diffusion processes in space.

2. Optical Spectrometer

Designed and constructed a functional hardware optical spectrometer. The build utilized a web camera, diffraction grating, convex lens, and precision slit to capture and analyze light spectra computationally.

3. 8-Bit Computer

Built a programmable 8-bit breadboard computer entirely from scratch. Inspired by Ben Eater's architecture, this project involved wiring logic gates, memory modules, and clock circuits to understand low-level computing and machine architecture.
