Computational astrophysics

Cosmic-ray propagation in the Galaxy

A numerical study of particle transport using Monte Carlo pitch-angle scattering and macroscopic advection–diffusion models. The workflow explores transport behaviour and compares particle-level intuition with PDE-based solutions.

  • Python
  • Monte Carlo
  • Crank–Nicolson
  • Numerical PDEs

Cosmology · Data analysis

Primordial helium abundance

A statistical analysis of published primordial helium abundance determinations, including robust central estimators, normalized residuals, and goodness-of-fit comparisons with candidate probability distributions.

  • Astrostatistics
  • Python
  • KS tests
  • Uncertainty analysis

Observational astronomy

Quantifying sunspots

Working with solar FITS images and astronomical image-analysis tools to identify and quantify sunspots using region selection and ellipse fitting.

  • FITS
  • SAOImage DS9
  • Image analysis

Experimental physics

Optical spectroscopy

Building and calibrating a low-cost diffraction-grating spectrometer, extracting spectral features from camera measurements, and examining wavelength calibration and resolution limits.

Selected projects

Cosmic Ray Propagation in the Galaxy

  • Developed 1D, 2D, and 3D simulations of cosmic-ray transport from supernova sources through the interstellar medium.
  • Used Monte Carlo methods to model pitch-angle scattering and random walks of 1500+ particles.
  • Solved the diffusion PDE using Crank–Nicolson and the Thomas algorithm; validated results statistically against analytical solutions.
  • Applied Dirichlet boundary conditions to study galactic escape and particle residence times.

Quantifying Sunspots using SAOImage DS9

  • Analysed solar FITS images to identify sunspots, measure their sizes, and estimate physical areas using image-analysis techniques.
  • Gained experience in astronomical data analysis and handling observational data.

Optical Spectrometer

  • Designed and constructed a low-cost diffraction-grating spectrometer with webcam-based detection, a slit mechanism, a light-isolation enclosure, and an alignment setup.
  • Captured and analysed spectral data, demonstrated wavelength separation and intensity measurement, and estimated the Sun’s temperature using Wien’s displacement law.

8-bit Breadboard Computer

  • Built and tested digital-logic modules including gates, registers, counters, and memory circuits to explore computer architecture and instruction execution.
